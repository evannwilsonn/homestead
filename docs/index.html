What moved the price

Compared with a typical Ames house at —: 1,440 sq ft, one story, built 1972, North Ames.

On 439 held-out sales the model was off by ±11.6% typically (R² 0.924). It is not a current market valuation.

The model

Ridge regression (λ = 30) on log price with 115 columns: 33 numeric features, six engineered ones like house age and total square footage, and 75 one-hot categories led by neighborhood. The fit is written from scratch in Julia and matches MLJ to 1e-14.

How it was tested

The data was split 70 / 15 / 15 and choices were made with 5-fold cross-validation. The final model was refit on train and validation, then scored once on 439 sales it had never seen: typical error ±11.6%, mean absolute error $13,805.

What it can't do

It knows Ames from 2006 to 2010 only. Its worst misses are cheap pre-1970 houses and non-market sales, such as allocations between family members. Inputs outside the training range get a warning above.
